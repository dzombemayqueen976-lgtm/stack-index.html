Undo Stack Editor

Every edit you make is pushed onto an undo stack. Undo pops the newest edit and pushes it onto a redo stack. Watch both stacks change as you work on the poster.

Stack operations

    Why a stack? The step you made last is the first one you want to reverse, and that is last in, first out. A queue would undo your oldest edit first, which would leave the poster in the wrong state.
